# MultiStreamPopout

Watch every stream in your voice channel at once, each in its own popout window.

Right click a stream, or the voice channel you're in, and click **Pop Out All Streams**.
Click a popout (or right click it → **Switch Stream**) to show a different stream in that window. The same stream can be shown in several windows.
Enable **Auto pop-out** in the settings to open new streams in their own window automatically.
